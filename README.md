# Study Quest

A free study app for IGCSE maths students: practice, timed exams, flashcards and progress tracking, plus over 2,000 real Edexcel past-paper questions sorted by topic, difficulty and tier (Foundation, Higher, accelerated), each linked to the official paper and mark scheme.

Open it here: https://walaabadran.github.io/study-quest/

The past papers and mark schemes are Pearson Edexcel's; the links go to the free copies on Physics & Maths Tutor, MyMathsCloud and other revision sites. Progress is saved in your own browser on your own device.
